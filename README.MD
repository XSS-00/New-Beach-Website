## 🌊 The three zones
 
| Zone | Vibe | Price |
|------|------|-------|
| ☂️ **Bango** | Relaxed, friendly spots close to the shore | 2,000 DA / person |
| 🌴 **Danzo** | Shaded premium zone with a great sea view | 3,000 DA / person |
| 👑 **Costa Fiesta** | Signature zone with upgraded sunbeds, VIP access and club atmosphere | Normal 4,000 DA · VIP 5,000 DA / person |

## ✨ Features
 
- 📅 **Online booking** in 5 simple steps, solo or for a group
- 📊 **Live availability** per zone, with used and remaining spots
- 🪑 **200 seats per day** tracked across the three zones
- 💵 **Cash payment** option
- 🌍 **Bilingual** interface: French and English
- 🚫 **No more on-site reservations**
